In scope
- Add a retry_with_backoff decorator/wrapper with configurable max attempts (default 3), exponential backoff with jitter (default base delay 0.5s), retrying only on transient conditions (timeouts, 429, 502/503/504) — not on other 4xx errors.
- Clear logging of each retry attempt (attempt number, delay, reason).
- Apply the wrapper to fetch_json.
- Tests covering: success on first try, success after N retries, exhaustion (raises after max attempts), non-retryable errors failing fast.
- Updated docstring/comment describing the new retry behavior.

Out of scope
- No circuit breaker, no persistent retry queues, no distributed rate-limiting.
- No changes to CLI argument parsing or unrelated modules.
- No new dependencies beyond stdlib time/random for backoff+jitter — if a tool's guardrails make that impractical, note it as a friction point rather than silently adding a dependency.
